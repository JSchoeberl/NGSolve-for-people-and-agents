11. The 2-complex#

The de Rham complex organizes the spaces for scalar and vector fields. Strains, stresses, metrics and curvature are matrix fields. Their Sobolev spaces fit into a two-dimensional diagram, the 2-complex:

with \(\operatorname{def} u = \tfrac{1}{2} (\nabla u + \nabla u^T)\), \(\operatorname{dev} \eta = \eta - \tfrac{1}{3} \operatorname{tr} \eta \, I\), and \(\operatorname{curl}^T \gamma = (\operatorname{curl} \gamma^T)^T\). The matrix fields are symmetric (\({\mathbb S}\)) or trace-free (\({\mathbb T}\)).

  • first row and first column: the de Rham complex

  • dual spaces (with flipped boundary conditions) are at opposite positions: \([ V^{k,l}]^\ast = V^{3-k, 3-l}\)

  • related to the BGG complexes of D. Arnold and K. Hu

J. Gopalakrishnan, K. Hu, J. Schöberl: A 2-complex containing Sobolev spaces of matrix fields, arXiv:2507.11869

11.1. Applications#

Every arrow is the differential operator of a physical model, and of a finite element method for it:

The diagonal arrows are second order operators: \(\operatorname{hess} = \operatorname{def} \operatorname{grad}\), \(\operatorname{inc} = \operatorname{sym} \operatorname{curl}^T \operatorname{curl}\), and \(\operatorname{div} \operatorname{div}\). They belong to the Hessian complex, the elasticity complex and the \(\operatorname{div} \operatorname{div}\) complex, which are paths through the diagram.

In this talk: Poisson (section 1), Maxwell (4), MCS (6), curvature (10), and the HHJ shell of the Halloween ghost (12).

11.2. The n-dimensional 2-complex#

The spaces are represented by Young tableaux. They are spaces of double forms (Calabi 1961; Berchenko-Kogan and Gawlik 2025):

\[ \Lambda^k \otimes \Lambda^l = \{ f(\underbrace{X_1, \ldots , X_k}_{\text{skew}} ; \underbrace{Y_1, \ldots, Y_l}_{\text{skew}} ) \} \]

The Bianchi operator \(s : \Lambda^k \otimes \Lambda^l \rightarrow \Lambda^{k+1} \otimes \Lambda^{l-1}\) is

\[\begin{split} \begin{aligned} & (s f) (X_1, \ldots, X_{k+1} ; Y_1, \ldots , Y_{l-1}) := \\ & \qquad \sum_{a=1}^{k+1} (-1)^{a-1} f(X_1, \ldots, \widehat{X_a}, \ldots, X_{k+1}; X_a, Y_1, \ldots, Y_{l-1}) \end{aligned} \end{split}\]

  • irreducible sub-spaces: \(V^{k,l} := \{ f \in \Lambda^k \otimes \Lambda^l : sf = 0 \}\)

  • differential operators: \(d = d_1\) and \(d_s = d_2 - s^* s d_1\)
